KEY FINDING ON DEFAULTS
MS-OI29500 has no note that changes a default for any of these: roundedCorners, varyColors, smooth, plotVisOnly, date1904, autoTitleDeleted, style, the c:CT_Boolean val default, gapWidth or overlap, crosses or crossBetween, tick marks, or the case where dispBlanksAs is absent.
Office 2007's "default false" notes for these are in MS-OE376 (see the section near the end), and every one of them is limited to Office 2007. So for Office 2010 and later, Microsoft documents no deviation from the schema defaults (c:CT_Boolean val default="true").
Neither document says what Office does when one of these elements is absent altogether. That has to be measured.

=== CHART SPACE AND CHART-WIDE SETTINGS ===
- 21.2.2.42 dispBlanksAs, 2.1.1452 oi/1942c028-3c4e-4dd9-a379-b12a9b7914df. A per-chart-type table: S = supported methods, D = the type's default method.
    barChart, bar3DChart: S gap, zero; D gap
    areaChart, grouping standard: S gap, zero; D gap
    areaChart, grouping not standard (stacked, percentStacked): S zero only; D zero
    area3DChart: S zero only; D zero
    lineChart, grouping standard: S gap, span, zero; D gap
    lineChart, grouping not standard: S zero only; D zero
    line3DChart: S gap, zero; D gap
    pieChart, pie3DChart, doughnutChart: S gap, zero; D gap
    bubbleChart, scatterChart, radarChart: S gap, span, zero; D gap
  An unsupported method falls back to the type's default.
  The note does not cover a missing dispBlanksAs element, and it does not list ofPie, stock or surface charts.
- 21.2.2.28 chartObject, 2.1.1445 oi/f0443747-f6a1-4f2b-b35a-3b4159cf25f9. Office ignores it and allows editing whatever the value.
- 21.2.2.37 data, 2.1.1449 oi/1ddb29af-9f99-49dd-b024-07491c8d02ef. Ignored. The user can still change the data.
- 21.2.2.72 formatting, 2.1.1472 oi/1a0d07f9-ff1b-4373-ae2a-fdc4610c6c36. Only partly respected.
- 21.2.2.149 protection, 2.1.1526 oi/8c074063-cd93-4739-91fd-ec17d2bfa679. Applies whether or not the sheet is protected. Office writes only the selection child; the other children are not implemented.
- 21.2.2.219 userInterface, 2.1.1567 oi/2e9f2f68-de8a-4769-81ec-c433df93c50c. Its presence means protection, whatever val says. Office never writes it, and on load treats it as standard protection.

- 21.2.2.148 printSettings, 2.1.1525 oi/ed56b005-235b-4130-8db9-602e08a13b96. If pageMargins is missing, Office creates one with application-defined values.
- 21.2.2.133 pageMargins, 2.1.1513 oi/b030ac61-0cab-41f3-a939-6a5c193f33f3. Each of b, t, l, r, header and footer must be at least 0 and less than 49. (g) Office swaps header and footer on save.

=== TITLE, AUTOTITLEDELETED AND MANUAL LAYOUT ===
- 21.2.2.7 autoTitleDeleted, 2.1.1431 oi/94796dcb-1512-4541-b4d0-9514d4070913. Applies only when the parent c:chart has no c:title. The note gives no default.
- 21.2.2.210 title, 2.1.1561 oi/a87b8094-484e-49fd-bdf7-ae3ed95e9572.
  (a) With no tx, the text is a localized string for the parent type. The exception: if the chart has a single series and that series has a name, the title is that name.
  (b) The title needs default or direct character properties (defRPr or rPr).
- 21.2.2.156 rich, 2.1.1533 oi/6f6e05f3-8a22-4170-a467-b859506d76c7. The p elements together may hold at most 255 characters.
- 21.2.2.216 txPr, 2.1.1566 oi/7567be8d-0d5e-4265-a80b-52a3006799b3. Ignored on load and removed on save if the sibling tx contains rich. Only the first a:p is read or saved.
- 21.2.2.104 manualLayout, 2.1.1494 oi/8ef04866-6150-457e-9997-509ec5a80cf5. Under legend or plotArea, x, y, w and h are all present or all absent. Under dispUnitsLbl, dLbl, title or trendlineLbl, x and y come together.
- 21.2.2.88 layout, 2.1.1485 oi/103a45d5-64c4-4e46-b972-658f87b24c63. Under dLbl or trendlineLbl, it is not saved unless it has a manualLayout, even when it has an extLst.
- 21.2.2.89 layoutTarget, 2.1.1486 oi/63d53b16-d3cf-4361-b048-75462a9a40e7. Not allowed under dispUnitsLbl, dLbl, legend, title or trendlineLbl.
- 21.2.2.78 h 2.1.1477 oi/2492a099-586a-4b75-99a5-39bb273e5c0a and 21.2.2.229 w 2.1.1573 oi/251f0661-860c-455e-bc94-a20c41df76c7. Ignored under dispUnitsLbl, dLbl, title or trendline, where the size fits the text. A val below 0 or above 1 makes Office ignore the whole manualLayout and use the default size and position.
- 21.2.2.232 x 2.1.1575 oi/39d7c733-19c2-4442-87d9-97c0efce4cda and 21.2.2.235 y 2.1.1577 oi/8c46e53b-c525-4bb6-8927-59d133a60a06. The whole manualLayout is ignored if val > 1, if val < 0 in edge mode, or if val < -1 in factor mode. An in-range value that would push the element outside the chart is moved inside, keeping its size.
- 21.2.2.81 hMode 2.1.1479 oi/4d59213a-27af-4901-9006-ff11f30589b4 and 21.2.2.231 wMode 2.1.1574 oi/67d2127f-6922-43a2-9ffe-e4d67615136d. Office writes only factor; edge is converted on save, with h and w recomputed. (wMode a) The mode applies to the parent element.
- 21.2.2.233 xMode 2.1.1576 oi/033c001f-a29a-4817-a32a-75394f971109 and 21.2.2.236 yMode 2.1.1578 oi/3e552fb6-fb17-40bd-aa1c-6628425e5b8a. Office writes only edge; factor is converted on save. After conversion x or y can fall outside 0..1, and the layout is then ignored on the next load.
- 21.2.3.20 ST_LayoutMode, 2.1.1587 oi/bb8565e7-dfea-453f-868b-ae680ab8a823.
  edge: h and w give the distance from the chart's top or left edge to the element's bottom or right edge; x and y give the element's left or top edge. All are fractions of the chart's size.
  factor: h and w give the element's size as a fraction of the chart's. x and y are offsets from the element's DEFAULT position, as fractions of the chart's size.
